THE GAP

Businesses do not lose leads because they lack tools. They lose them between tools.

Most SMB lead stacks are fragmented across ad platforms, forms, messaging apps, spreadsheets, CRMs and manual follow-up. Context disappears at every transition.

01

Fragmented acquisition

Lead sources deliver different payloads, identifiers and attribution metadata with no shared operating model.

02

Manual qualification

Sales teams repeatedly ask the same questions because qualification logic lives in people instead of infrastructure.

03

Broken feedback loops

Marketing knows the cost per lead. Sales knows what closed. The two systems rarely share a reliable event trail.

ARCHITECTURE

A neutral operations layer around the lead lifecycle.

LeadOps keeps the core domain portable. Providers become adapters, business rules stay inspectable, and outbound actions remain replaceable.

Explainability first
